# Privacy

incident-rca is instructions only. It has no server, no MCP connector, no hooks, and makes no network calls.

- It reads only the files and text you give it in your Claude session.
- It collects, stores, and sends nothing to the author or any third party.
- The postmortem it writes is saved only to your own machine.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ashishjsharda/incident-rca/issues
